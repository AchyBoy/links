MOONBERRY MANOR — CHAPTER 3: THE NAMES WE LEAVE BEHIND (DRAFT v1, NOT APPROVED, NOT BUILT)

Mostly night. About 35-40 minutes, with a 15-20 minute dungeon. Boss: the Forgotten Giant. New friend: Boo.

Heart of it: forgetting hurts less, but it takes the good part too. Ch2 asked Kit to remember one person. Ch3 asks who remembers everybody else.

Cite any number to change it ("change #042"). Numbers cover dialogue, stage, choices and gameplay.

ACT II — THE QUIET HALLS (dungeon, 15-20 min)

#052 GAMEPLAY Enemies: Hushlings, round grey shapes that swallow sound. Every attack glows and marks the ground first. Music and footsteps go soft when one is near.

#053 STAGE A round hall. Left: a passage of old bells. Right: a smooth stone door with no handle. Straight ahead, too far to jump: a ledge with a hook ring and a chest.

#054 Kit No handle. No keyhole.

#055 Boo It only opens for people who are listening.

#056 Kit I am listening.

#057 Boo Not with those ears.

#058 GAMEPLAY The right door can't be opened yet. The ledge is the come-back-later bonus (grappling hook, later chapter). Same rules as Whisper Cave.

Left branch: Hall of Bells

#059 GAMEPLAY Room 1: hang three fallen bells back on their hooks while Hushlings try to muffle them.

#060 GAMEPLAY Room 2: the bells repeat a five-note tune. Play it back. If the player has Grandma's songbook, the notes show on screen.

#061 Hero [songbook] It's her song. The moth and the moon.

#062 Mittens [songbook] She sang it here too, then.

#063 Boo [no songbook] She used to hum it. Dum, dum, da, dum... I lost the end.

#064 GAMEPLAY Room 3: dark gallery. Moth Charm lights the safe path; without it, Boo lights one step at a time (slower).

#065 GAMEPLAY Mini-boss: the Great Hush. It eats the room's sound; ringing a bell stuns it. Three rings to win. Both Walker sides can win.

#066 STAGE Behind it, Grandma's old stool, a cup, and a page pinned under a little brass bell.

#067 Grandma When you don't know what to say, little bat, be quiet with someone. Listening is a kind of holding hands.

#068 Hero That's her handwriting.

#069 GAMEPLAY New power: LISTEN. Stand still and hold the button: hidden bells, voices and doors light up. Works at any spot on the Day/Night meter. Stays learned.

#070 Kit What do you hear?

#071 Hero The door. It's been saying hello this whole time.

SECRETS (3)

S1 | Grandma's Evening Cloak (first "Better" cloak), behind a wall only Listen reveals in the Hall of Bells.

S2 | Silver Rapier (long reach, medium damage) in the dark gallery, off the main path.

S3 | The white flower: visit the small stone on a later night and leave a flower of your own. No reward now; Elias remembers it in a later chapter.

AFTER THE CHAPTER (free play)

- The hill stays open. The Quiet Halls can be replayed for XP and moon drops and reset each time, like Whisper Cave.

- Visit the hill on any later night to read the names. Boo hums if you found the songbook.

KEPT OUT ON PURPOSE

Grandma's fate, Thomas's fate, Boo's real name and Elias's daughter's name all stay unknown. No biting in this chapter (nobody on the hill has blood). Moon meter (limit break), the grappling hook and the fight you're meant to lose wait for later chapters.
