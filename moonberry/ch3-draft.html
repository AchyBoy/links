MOONBERRY MANOR — CHAPTER 3: THE NAMES WE LEAVE BEHIND (v4: ChatGPT's third review merged 11-24, NOT BUILT)

Mostly night. About 35-40 minutes, with a 15-20 minute dungeon. Boss: the Forgotten Giant. New friend: Boo.

Heart of it: forgetting hurts less, but it takes the good part too. Ch2 asked Kit to remember one person. Ch3 asks who remembers everybody else.

Cite any number to change it ("change #042"). Numbers cover dialogue, stage, choices and gameplay.

SECRETS (3)

S1 | Grandma's Evening Cloak (first "Better" cloak), behind a wall only Listen reveals in the Hall of Bells.

S2 | Silver Rapier (long reach, medium damage) in the dark gallery, off the main path.

S3 | The white flower: visit the small stone on a later night and leave a flower of your own. No XP, coins, popup or reward; Elias remembers it in a later chapter. Her stone is separate from the lanterns and her name never comes back in this chapter.

AFTER THE CHAPTER (free play)

- The hill stays open. The Quiet Halls' enemies and puzzles reset for XP and moon drops, like Whisper Cave.

- NEVER reset: returned memories, the Giant's recovered identity (calm way only), Boo living at the manor, the third key, the white flower, trust changes.

- Boo in ordinary play: curious, enthusiastic, sometimes forgetful, delighted by little things. No jokes inside the big emotional scenes. Boo does not keep talking about forgetting.

- Visit the hill on any later night to read the names. Boo hums if you found the songbook.

- Later idea, not needed for this release: after a fight, return the lanterns and come back to help the Giant remember.

- The pocket watch is optional everywhere. Its louder ticking needs a payoff in a later chapter.

SAVE FLAGS (tracked separately)

Third key collected. Giant calmed OR defeated. Giant's identity restored (calm way only). Spirits' memories returned (and whether the lanterns were kept first). Returning the memories and then fighting the Giant anyway is allowed and stays consistent.

Mrs. Wren's apology: checked in the game 11-24, there is no earlier way to apologize, so #007-#011 stand.

FIXED NAMES (same in the stones, lanterns, dialogue, book and free play)

Fiddler = Jasper. Fisher = Nell. Baker = Otto. Not random. Never shown or heard on the kept-lanterns path until they are returned.

KEPT OUT ON PURPOSE

Grandma's fate, Thomas's fate, Boo's real name and Elias's daughter's name all stay unknown. No biting in this chapter (nobody on the hill has blood). Moon meter (limit break), the grappling hook and the fight you're meant to lose wait for later chapters.
