CHAPTER 4, VERSION 2

MOONBERRY MANOR: CHAPTER 4, THE PRICE OF HUNGER (v2, 11-24, checked by a second reader, NOT APPROVED, NOT BUILT)

Starts in daylight, ends at night. About 40 minutes, with a 20 minute dungeon. Boss: the Hollow King. New friends: Crumb and Old Ratwick.

Heart of it: everyone is a little hungry, and hungry isn't bad. What matters is who gets to sit at the table. Ch3 asked who remembers everybody. Ch4 asks who feeds everybody when nobody is looking.

Lighter than Ch3: more jokes, a lost little market, and a proud King whose stomach is louder than he is.

Cite any number to change it ("change #042"). Numbers cover dialogue, stage, choices and gameplay.

ACT II: THE WATER WORKS (dungeon, 20 min for a person)

#063 GAMEPLAY THE IDEA: one big place, three water levels. Blue, green and gold wheels set the water LOW, MIDDLE or HIGH everywhere. Low opens tunnels, middle floats rafts, high lifts you to ledges. You pass the same halls more than once and they change each time. Picture signs and water color show the level; no reading needed.

#064 GAMEPLAY BUILD TARGET: at least 21 rooms and at least 14 minutes of BOT time for a full clear (Whisper Cave was 8.6, the Quiet Halls 6.8, both short). Length comes from rooms, routes and returning, never from waiting or bigger health bars. Two shortcuts back to Undertown open along the way. Leaving and fainting keep progress.

#065 GAMEPLAY Enemies: Nibblers, round dust-grey fluffballs with one big tooth that chew lamps and baskets. Drips, hopping water blobs that push you. Rust Crabs, slow, armored in front, turn them with a Block or walk around. Every attack glows and marks the ground first. Nibblers can be bitten or let go like the moths, so Night Walkers can heal here.

#066 STAGE Entry hall. A dead water wheel as tall as a house. Left: a dry channel. Right: a flooded arch. High above, out of reach, a ledge with a hook ring and a chest.

#067 Boo There's a chest up there. I said hello. It's locked, or shy.

#068 GAMEPLAY The ledge is the come-back-later bonus (grappling hook, later). Grandma's Stone Knock opens a shorter way through the first hall if learned in Whisper Cave. Fog Form slips the bars to a side chest. Neither is needed.
