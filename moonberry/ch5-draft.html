MOONBERRY MANOR: CHAPTER 5, WHEN THE BELLS FELL SILENT (DRAFT v3, 11-26: second GPT review merged. STRUCTURE APPROVED. LAYOUT ONLY, NOTHING BUILT)

Day and night. About 70 minutes, the longest chapter so far: Boo's half (about 30 min), the tower (about 30 min, including a 20 minute dungeon), the fall and the morning after (about 10 min). Boss: the Last Clockkeeper (fight only). New: friend rescues.

Heart of it: Ch3 asked who remembers everybody. Ch4 asked who feeds everybody. Ch5 asks what you would give so somebody else can stay.

Boo gets three wishes. They are things she really wants, not a list to finish before she goes. She has every reason to stay: she loves her friends, the manor and the adventures. That is what makes it a sacrifice. Her last wish becomes a way to help the people she loves, and she chooses it.

Most of her time in this chapter is NOT foreshadowing. Let her be silly, laugh and annoy Mittens. The player has to MISS her, not just feel sad.

Boo is brave AND still wants to stay. She'd happily have had a hundred more adventures. Her wishes are not getting ready to leave. Her choice is an extraordinary answer to a magical emergency, not something children are meant to copy; the rest of the game keeps celebrating friendship, everyday kindness, courage and being alive. Don't make it sadder than this. The more fun the first half is, the less the ending needs to say.

Rules: Boo is a girl (about six). Boo's sacrifice happens BEFORE the Kit or key choice and is the same on every path, every ending, every save. Nothing ever undoes it. Gentle: she glows brighter and becomes the bell's ringing. Nobody is hurt on screen.

Cite any number to change it ("change #042"). Numbers cover dialogue, stage, choices and gameplay.

SECRETS (3)

S1 The attic: a child's drawing of a bell signed with a wobbly B, in the rocking horse's saddle bag. Put it in Grandma's book. After the tower, it sits next to her name. No XP or popup.

S2 The tower, floor 4: a dusty side room with Thomas's old hunting whistle. Give it to Kit before the bell room: one Kit line ("He'd whistle this when supper was ready.") and Kit's rescue lasts a little longer.

S3 The sunrise: if the Chapter 3 memories were returned, Jasper plays Boo a song on the hill. If the player found Grandma's songbook, it's the moth and the moon song, and Boo hums along. A line in the Tuesday book.

AFTER THE CHAPTER (free play)

- The tower ruins stay all season. Flowers renew every morning. The ghosts visit at dusk.

- Boo is gone for good. Her sacrifice can't be prevented by any alignment, item, secret, ending or replayed dungeon.

- Her helper slot stays as a small remembrance (her name and a little bell drawing), never a lock icon, never anything that looks like a character waiting to unlock.

- Friend rescues work without her. Every earlier puzzle can be finished without her. No later chapter needs her helper ability. Review all of Boo's upgrades, controls and quests. Every earlier place that needed her glow or phasing must still be finishable. No other friend gets all her abilities straight away; the party works differently without her. Moon drops and moth dust spent on her upgrades come back quietly, with no popup or reward sound.

- Chapter 6 does not hand out a new companion straight away. Her absence is felt in small moments: a dark spot where she used to light the way, a joke nobody makes, Kit looking at the sill. Not constant sadness.

- Mittens sleeps on the sill now.

- Tap the Tuesday book on the shelf: Bea's page. Tap Grandma's book: both names.

- The attic stays open for its chests.

PACING

- About 70 minutes. If it has to be shorter after playtesting, cut repeated dungeon floors first. Never cut Boo's wishes, the hide and seek or the morning after.

- Natural save points: after the opening, after the sunrise, after the attic, at the tower door, before the boss.

SAVE FLAGS (separate)

Boo's wishes 1, 2, 3. boo_gone (always, every path). Fifth key: key_secured OR kit_saved (key taken by the lantern light). Kit trust change on B. Thomas's coat. Thomas's letter read. Attic opened. Friend rescues unlocked. Friend upgrade ranks. Secrets S1 to S3. Names flags from Ch3 only read, never changed.

KEPT OUT ON PURPOSE

Grandma stays missing; nothing says alive or dead. Elias is not fought, and he is shaken but not changed yet (Ch7 is his last chance). The shadow spirit is not named or shown. Nobody is hurt on screen; Kit's sling is the worst of it. Thomas is confirmed gone, gently: he gave the tower all his time and can never come home. That is the ANSWER this chapter gives.
