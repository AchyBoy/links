🦎 Leo, care plan + tracker

Started 27 Nov 2026. Leopard gecko, young.
Where it started (27 Nov): barely eating about a month (some random bites, last ~2 weeks), tail getting skinnier, gums bled once, legs maybe a little wobbly. Mouth looked healthy pink in the photo, no white gunk, jaw not soft.
Main cause found: tank is too COLD. Floor right under the 75W lamp read 74.8°F (needs 88 to 92). Air midway up read 81. Cool side 73.9.

Shopping list

  • ☐ Heat mat about 11" x 17" (iPower / Fluker's / Zoo Med), ~$20 to 30
  • ☐ Thermostat, Inkbird ITC-306A or similar, ~$35 (never run the mat without it)
  • ☐ Slate tile about 12x12, flat and smooth (Home Depot / Lowe's / pet store)
  • ☐ Two outlet timers (or one double), heat lamp + UVB
  • ☐ Maybe: spare 75W basking bulb
  • ☐ Maybe: fresh Repashy Calcium Plus if the jar is over ~6 months open
  • ☐ Maybe later: plexiglass or plastic cutting board to cover part of the mesh top

Tonight (until the mat is in)

  • Make sure the 75W bulb is in the heat lamp
  • Slate on top of the grass mat, right under the lamp
  • Heat lamp stays ON overnight for now (it is his only heat)
  • UVB OFF at night
  • Keep his room as warm as you can

Setup day

  1. Stick the heat mat under the glass on the lamp side, about a third of the floor
  2. Cut the grass mat out over that spot, lay the slate right on the glass
  3. Put a hide on or next to the slate
  4. Mat into thermostat, probe flat on the slate, set 90°F
  5. Heat lamp + UVB on timers, about 8am on, 8pm off
  6. Mat stays on 24/7, the thermostat controls it
  7. If still low: cover part of the mesh top, away from the lamps, leave some open for air. Never cover over or around the lamps.

Target temps (probe flat on the floor)

  • Warm spot, day: 88 to 92°F
  • Cool side: 72 to 78°F
  • Night: warm spot stays warm from the mat, air can drop to ~70

Calcium + vitamins

  • Dish: plain Repti Calcium (no D3) only, always out. Stop mixing Repashy into it.
  • Most feedings: dust bugs with Repti Calcium
  • Once a week: dust with Repashy Calcium Plus instead
  • Reptivite: not needed (same job as Repashy, use one)
  • UVB on in the day helps him make his own D3

Feeding

  • Give him one day at proper heat first
  • Then 2 or 3 small dusted bugs every other day
  • Waxworm treat to tempt him, 1 or 2 only
  • Take out uneaten bugs by morning
Take him to a reptile vet if:
  • still not eating after a week at proper heat
  • tail keeps getting thinner, or weaker / more wobbly
  • gums bleed again, or white cheesy stuff in the mouth
